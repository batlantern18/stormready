# StormReady 1.2.0

**First public release**

StormReady 1.2.0 is a local-first emergency preparedness app for households in the United States and Mexico. It helps a household make a plan, keep supplies current, coordinate with one another, and review official preparedness guidance without requiring an account.

## Highlights

- **Household planning:** Set household details, preparedness days, pets, medical needs, accessibility needs, and an evacuation zone during onboarding.
- **Supply tracking:** Record quantities, purchase dates, expiration dates, notes, and seasonal rotations for core emergency supplies.
- **Go-bags:** Organize grab-and-go items by household member and see what is ready or still missing.
- **Medical planning:** Record medications, refill dates, refrigerated items, backup power needs, and medical contacts. Optional local refill reminders can be scheduled.
- **Communication plans:** Save contacts, meeting places, and check-in instructions that can be shared without an account.
- **Preparedness drills:** Run offline scenarios to identify gaps and turn them into practical next steps.
- **Readiness summary:** Review preparedness progress and seasonal priorities from one screen.
- **Map and local guidance:** Find nearby shelters, verify evacuation zones, save an offline map area, and use offline route guidance within saved road coverage.
- **Plan transfer:** Export a household plan or transfer it to another device with a QR code. Transfers stay between the devices involved.
- **English and Spanish:** Switch languages from Settings; dates follow the selected language.
- **Light and dark mode:** The interface adapts to the device appearance with readable contrast in both modes.
- **Privacy controls:** Review stored app data, clear it, and keep personal plan information on the device unless you choose to share or export it.

## Important limitations

- StormReady is a planning aid, not an emergency alert authority. Follow current instructions from local emergency management.
- Shelter availability and capacity should be confirmed with local authorities before traveling.
- Automated evacuation-zone lookup is currently available for Florida. Elsewhere, use the zone published by the local emergency authority.
- Offline routes work only inside the road coverage saved on the device and do not include live traffic, closures, or turn restrictions.
- New map downloads, address search, shelter refreshes, and some official data lookups require an internet connection.
- Location is requested only after a location-based action is selected.
