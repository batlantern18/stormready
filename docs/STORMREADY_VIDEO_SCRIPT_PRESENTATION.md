# StormReady — Project Presentation Video Script

**Style:** Founder-narrated, fast-paced, timestamped (same format as the original app-script).
**Target runtime:** ~2:30–3:00
**Based on:** App purpose, audience, features, inspiration, technical challenges, learnings, and v2.0 vision.

---

## PRESENTATION SCRIPT

**[0:00–0:07]**
Every year, millions of families are one hurricane away from chaos.

**[0:07–0:15]**  
Hurricanes. When the moment comes, half the plan was written long before —in the forgotten checklist that never got finished.

**[0:15–0:24]**  
I built StormReady. And I believe that preparedness can help alievate pressure in hurricanes.

**[0:24–0:33]**  
StormReady is a offline preparedness app for households in the Florida. It is mainly meant for hurricanes — helping a household build an emergency plan, maintain a real supply kit, organize go-bags, coordinate with family, and check official local guidance.

**[0:33–0:42]**  
The interference is simple with No account. No cloud. No tracking. Your plan stays on your device, offline first.

**[0:42–0:52]**
So who is this for? Households preparing for hurricanes and other emergencies — families, caregivers, pet owners, people with medical needs — especially in hurricane-prone Florida.

**[0:52–1:05]**
Here's what the app does. It tracks inventory, quantities, expiration dates, and rotations.

**[1:05–1:18]**
It builds go-bags by person and saves contacts, meeting places, and check-ins.

**[1:18–1:30]**
It runs offline drills and reviews preparedness at a glance.

**[1:30–1:45]**
It plans medical needs — medications, refills, backup power — and for hurricane season in Florida, finds shelters, verifies evacuation zones, saves offline map areas, and gets route guidance when it matters most.

**[1:45–2:00]**
And it surfaces weather and safety alerts, so guidance stays current.

**[2:00–2:12]**  
I built this to help Florida, hurricane season turns that checklist into urgent, life-saving preparation.

**[2:12–2:26]**
The technical challenge was building local-first: offline maps, routing, versioned storage with migrations and corruption recovery, MapLibre native builds, and Expo SDK 58 breaking changes — including automated evacuation-zone lookup for Florida — all addressed through disciplined architecture.

**[2:26–2:40]**
The biggest lesson: local-first, privacy-first design is achievable and essential. Preparedness is a process, not a one-time checklist.

**[2:40–2:52]**
And this is just the beginning. Version 2.0 would automate evacuation-zone coverage beyond Florida, add official alert push integration, verify shelter capacity, expand languages, and release on iOS.

**[2:52–3:00]**
StormReady. Preparedness, made personal.

**[3:00–3:08]**
StormReady is a planning aid, not an emergency alert authority. Follow current directions from local emergency management, and verify shelter availability before traveling.
