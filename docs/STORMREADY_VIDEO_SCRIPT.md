# StormReady — Video Script

**Style reference:** Same format/voice as the Cognitive Care Assistant video script.
**Target runtime:** ~75 seconds

---

**[0:00 second]**
Every year, millions of families are one emergency away from chaos.

**[0:07 seconds]**
Storms. Floods. Wildfires. When the moment comes, half the story was written long before — in the drawer, in the forgotten checklist, in the plan that never got finished.

**[0:19 seconds]**
We are StormReady. And we believe preparedness is a checklist, not an app.

**[0:27 seconds]**
StormReady is a local-first preparedness app for households in the United States and Mexico.

**[0:35 seconds]**
It helps a household build an emergency plan, maintain a real supply kit, organize go-bags, coordinate with family, and check official local guidance.

**[0:50 seconds]**
The interface is simple and intuitive, all on one screen. No account. No cloud. No tracking. Your plan stays on your device, offline first.

**[1:01 seconds]**
Track inventory, quantities, expiration dates, rotations. Build go-bags by person. Save contacts, meeting places, check-ins.

**[1:12 seconds]**
Run offline drills. Review preparedness at a glance. See what needs attention, before it does.

**[1:21 seconds]**
Find shelters, verify evacuation zones, save offline map areas, and get route guidance when it matters most.

**[1:35 seconds]**
StormReady. Preparedness, made personal.

**[1:40 seconds]**
StormReady is a planning aid, not an emergency alert authority. Follow current directions from local emergency management, and verify shelter availability before traveling.

---

## Notes for production

- **Pacing:** Read each line briskly (~150 words/min), like the reference script.
- **On-screen text suggestions:**
  - "Local-first. No account. No tracking."
  - "Checklist → Plan"
  - "Track → Drill → Go"
- **Closing disclaimer** should appear on screen as end card text.
